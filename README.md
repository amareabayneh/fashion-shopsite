Anjones Fashion Shop

A Django-based e-commerce platform designed to support online fashion shopping, product catalog management, customer accounts, order processing, and digital payment workflows.

Project Overview

Anjones Fashion Shop is an online shopping platform designed to connect customers with fashion products through a user-friendly web interface.

The system provides a foundation for managing fashion products, displaying product information, organizing shopping activities, and handling customer orders. It is designed to support the major workflows involved in operating an online fashion store.

The project demonstrates my skills in Python, Django, database-driven application development, responsive web design, e-commerce workflows, and payment integration.

Main Features

- Product Catalog: Display fashion products with relevant descriptions and images.
- Product Categories: Organize products into categories for easier navigation.
- Product Details: Present product information, pricing, and available options.
- Product Search: Help customers discover products.
- Shopping Cart: Support the collection and review of selected products before checkout.
- Customer Accounts: Provide customer registration, login, and account management.
- Order Management: Support order placement and order-status tracking.
- Payment Integration: Integrate or prepare payment workflows for supported payment providers.
- Customer Management: Manage customer information and shopping activities.
- Product Management: Enable authorized staff to manage product listings and availability.
- Administrative Dashboard: Support the management of products, customers, and orders.
- Responsive Design: Provide a user interface suitable for desktop and mobile browsers.
- Multilingual Support: Support English and Amharic interfaces where implemented.
